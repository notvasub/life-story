# Community Garden Essay

When our neighborhood garden lost its irrigation system, I organized six volunteers to carry water every Saturday. I first tried assigning every task myself, but the youngest volunteer noticed that our route doubled back across the same beds. We mapped a shorter route together and cut watering time in half. The experience taught me that leadership is often listening closely enough for someone else to improve the plan.
